# v4.2: dead ball before the kick-off (2026-10-31)

## Problem in v4.1

Half 2 is graded from frame 110,808, but the kick-off is at frame 113,742 (the organisers' FAQ
names this trap). All 2,934 frames in between are dead ball. The decoder started every half in a
live state, and v4.1 labelled 1,294 of those frames H or A, which created spurious possessions.

## Idea (`sb/features.py: kickoff_frame`, `sb/v4.py`)

By the laws, the ball is not in play before the kick-off. We detect the kick-off from the tracking
alone; the ball is not tracked at this kick-off, so it is not used. The signature has three parts:

- Both teams stand in their own halves: at least 70% of each team's tracked players on its side.
- They are nearly still: team speed below 1.0 m/s for 2 s.
- Play then bursts: team speed above 1.5 m/s within 3 s.

The kick-off frame is where the speed first rises 0.25 m/s above the standstill.

When a half's graded window opens more than 10 s before that frame, the frames before it get strong
dead-ball evidence (logit +20), and the half starts in the D state. Half 1 opens on its kick-off:
it is detected at frame 11,188, 3 frames after the window opens, so half 1 is unchanged.

The mask test cannot measure the change: the public window lies in half 1. The gain comes from the
ground truth's own definition: those frames are dead, and three spurious possessions are gone.
