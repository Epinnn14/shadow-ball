# v3 — fouls & stoppages without the ball going out

## Problem in v2
- 89 dead-ball episodes, 40.6 minutes in total out of ~106 minutes of scored play (ball in play ~65 minutes).
  Rough benchmark (common estimate, not yet checked against a source): top leagues ~55 minutes of ball in play.
- 55/59 Smart Tagging set pieces are already caught; the ones missed are mainly fouls (the ball stays
  inside the pitch, so there is no "ball went out" evidence).
- Example in the public window (frames 15889-16399): after the whistle, the players' average speed drops
  2.6 -> 1.3 m/s within ~3 s, then stays at ~1.0 for 20 s; v2 only gives P(D) ~0.45.

## Idea (sb/features.py, sb/v3.py)
- `whistle_until_restart`: collective slowdown > 0.8 m/s within 3 s, ending < 1.6 m/s, and
  STAYING < 1.4 m/s for 4 s (confirmation) -> treated as dead until the ball is placed and then
  kicked, or the team clearly moves fast again, or 40 s. Weight 2.5 (strong evidence, not
  certainty like the ball going out, which has weight 8).
- 10 s window activity (stoppages are long; slow build-up is not that slow).
- Ball stationary inside the pitch for ~2 s while the team is slow (free-kick preparation), weight 1.5.
- Team control stays the same as in v2 (including the reconstructed ball).

## Decision
v3 becomes the main submission (27 Oct 2026): ranking metric on par with v2, tie-break (macroF1) clearly
better, full-match statistics more realistic. Honest note: spellF1 has not gone up; the public
window has only 4 stoppages, so the effect on full-match spellF1 cannot be measured here.
One public foul example was used to understand the pattern (not to choose parameter values).
