# v4 — ball in play from the restart kick

## Problem in v3
Transition diagnosis in the public window: after a dead ball, v3 only treats the ball as in play
1-5 s AFTER the actual restart (late by 24, 62, 112, 129 frames). Cause: the
"slow players" evidence uses a 5-10 s window, and right after a throw-in / goal
kick the players really are still slow.

## Idea
If a dead-ball latch (ball out, or inferred whistle) ends BECAUSE the ball
is kicked (median speed > 5 m/s), that is a visible restart. For the 5 s after it,
the dead-ball logit is reduced by 5 (strong "in play" evidence). Latches that end because of the time limit or
because the team becomes fast again are not counted as restarts.
77 restart kicks detected in the full match.
