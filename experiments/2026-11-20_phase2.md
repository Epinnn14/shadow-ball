# Half-time screen and chemistry on open game 2 (2026-11-20)

The four ideas saved for Phase 2, built after Látigo AI missed the top 20, on Metrica open game 2
(clip 55'-70'). Code: `sb/phase2.py`, `demo.build_phase2`, the `#htSection` block in `demo/`.
Hosted copy: `python scripts/build_site.py --game METRICA-2`.

## What each card uses (no look-ahead)

| Card | Data | Window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan (grades + suggestions) | offball_team, pressing, transitions, entries, offball_leak | first half only |
| Possession and threat | possession labels, ball, threat surface | completed minutes up to now |
| Heatmaps | ball positions per team | first half |
| Passing network | Metrica event data (PASS, BALL LOST, SHOT) | first half |
| Chemistry | tracking + roles | this half, kick-off to the clip's first frame |

The squad's XI (anonymous "Player <shirt>") is also taken from this half before the clip, and on the
open games the "km since kick-off" of a role counts the first-half running of the player who holds
it (slots are real players there; on the competition match slots are tracklets, so that path is
unchanged).

## Output on game 2

- Grades, home vs away: space denied 80% vs 79%, rest defence 88% vs 85%, possession 52% vs 48%,
  threat created 11.8 vs 10.5, pressing wins it back 22% vs 24%, counter-press 11% vs 5%,
  final-third entries 29 vs 29.
- Attack: "Keep using the left" (14 of 29 entries), "Go fast when we win it" (32% of 37 regains
  reached the final third within 10 s).
- Defend: "Close our right side" (16 of their 29 entries), "Keep pressing in their half" (24% won
  back vs 11% holding).
- Passing, first half: home 295 passes, 82% kept; away 243, 80%.
- Chemistry at 55': home 60%, away 68% (15 neighbour pairs each). Weakest home pair RB-RCB 47%.

## Validation status: the core metrics on all three open games

Same tests as on the competition match (`sb/offball.py`), top vs bottom quartile within ball zone x
team, weighted over strata. No parameter was changed for these games.

| Game | Space denied: entry within 10 s, high vs low | strata better | Rest defence: entry against within 15 s, high vs low | strata better | Players behind the ball (naive), high vs low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Competition match | 20.8% vs 30.9% | stratified | 6.1% vs 12.9% | 6/6 | fails |
| Metrica 1 | 27.3% vs 38.0% | 5/6 | 6.6% vs 14.1% | 5/6 | 12.0% vs 8.5% (wrong way) |
| Metrica 2 | 28.1% vs 43.7% | 6/6 | 7.7% vs 10.3% | 4/6 | 11.1% vs 9.3% (wrong way) |
| Metrica 3 | 15.3% vs 24.6% | 6/6 | 4.3% vs 14.9% | 4/6 | 10.6% vs 10.7% (none) |

Both metrics replicate on every game; the simple count of players behind the ball does not.
Note: the threat surface is learned per game from that game's own tracking (P(box within 10 s)),
as on the competition match. It uses no labels, but it is not out of sample.

Plans on the other games (as the app shows them at 55'):

- Game 1: attack "Go fast when we win it", "Keep using the left", "Play through their press";
  defend "Keep pressing in their half", "Close our left side".
- Game 3: attack "Attack their RCM" (11 per mille more space than in his first 15 min), "Go fast
  when we win it", "Keep using the left"; defend "Keep pressing in our third", "Close our right
  side", "Tighten up again".

Caveats:

- The plan's rules use these metrics with fixed thresholds (pressing difference > 5 points with
  15+ seconds each, leak growth > 4 per mille, lane share >= 40%). The leak threshold was raised
  from 2 to 4 per mille after seeing game 2 suggest leaks that were within noise; games 1 and 3
  (above) were run after that change. They describe the first half; they do not predict.
- Chemistry is not validated. It is shown as an indicator with its three parts so a coach can
  judge it.
