# v2 — ball reconstruction (ballnet) + v1

## Data findings
- The ball is missing in 150 gaps within the scored frames; median gap 10.7 s, 90% < 34 s.
- 85% of missing frames are in gaps > 10 s: interpolation alone is not enough.

## Idea
- Small model (~100k parameters): transformer over the player set (slot order does not matter) +
  one context token (last/next visible ball + its time offset).
- Learns a correction to a prior (interpolation / known endpoint / centre of the pitch).
- Self-supervised: frames with the ball visible, ball hidden with synthetic gaps whose
  lengths are drawn from the real gaps.
- v2 uses the reconstructed ball only for team-control evidence, weighted by exp(-sigma / 8 m).

## Initial results (CPU, 2,000 steps, Claude workspace)

| Validation | Model | Interpolation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cross (train half 1, test half 2), median error | 5.65 m | 5.75 m |
| Cross, with no ball context at all | 15.3 m | 33.8 m |

Loss is still dropping: full training (20,000 steps) on K's GPU.
The public 5-minute GT cannot evaluate v2 (the ball is almost always visible in that window): the main
measures for v2 are cross-half validation + `team_agreement` (nearest team according to the guessed ball vs
the real ball, when a player is < 3 m from the ball).

## GPU run 1 (failed: overfit) — `ballnet_20261024_2038_overfit_no_augment.json`
20,000 steps without augmentation/dropout: the model memorized the training half. Cross-validation
8.8-9.1 m (worse than interpolation at 5.6 m), nearest team correct 56% (interpolation 72%),
only 5% of errors within 1 sigma (overconfident). Lesson: adjacent frames are nearly identical,
so the ~47 thousand frames per half are really only a few thousand distinct situations.

## GPU run 2 (succeeded) — `ballnet_20261024_2123.json`
Added augmentation (mirror along pitch length/width, team swap), dropout 0.1, weight decay
1e-2, early stopping on the validation half.

| Cross-validation (average of both directions) | Model | Interpolation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Median error | 2.9 m | 5.6 m |
| Gap 0-2 s | 0.16 m | 0.16 m |
| Gap 2-10 s | 4.0 m | 6.9 m |
| Gap 10-30 s | 5.6 m | 17.0 m |
| Gap > 30 s | 6.5 m | 28.6 m |
| No ball context | 7.7 m | 32.8 m |
| Nearest team correct (player < 3 m) | 77% | 72% |
| Error within 1 sigma | 50% | - |

Early stopping picked the last step (19,500 and 20,000): the model can still be trained longer.

v2 over one match: 259 possessions, average 15.1 s, D share 38.3% (v1: 239, 16.3 s, 38.8%).

## Ball-hidden test (`scripts/mask_test.py`)
The ball in the public window is randomly hidden up to ~41% (as in the full match), v1 vs v2
scored against the public GT, 8 random maskings (paired comparison).

| | v1 | v2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average spellF1 | 0.419 | 0.451 (better 6/8, equal 2/8) |
| Average macroF1 | 0.686 | 0.723 (better 8/8) |

Note: the full model was also trained on the public-window frames, so the numbers above are optimistic.
