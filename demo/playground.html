        Player

        Pace scales his top speed (1.00× = 5 m/s) in the model and the match. Passing changes how often his passes arrive, finishing how often his shots go in; both count in the full match only. Squads carry no ratings, so everyone starts at 1.00×: rate the players yourself.

        How the match works

        Each second both teams take the shape of their formation and style, shifted with the ball. The player on the ball weighs passes, through balls, carries, crosses and shots by how much they raise the chance of reaching the box (learned from open tracking data) against the risk of losing it. Shot quality (xG) depends on distance, pressure and bodies in the way.

        A tactics toy, not a prediction: squads carry no ratings, so every player is equal apart from the pace you set, and a different kick-off gives a different match.

        How to read it

        Dangerous space is pitch control (who gets to each spot first) weighted by how often the ball reaches the box from there, learned from three open Metrica games. With the ball: the share you own, and rest defence, the share you would still hold if you lost it now. Without it: space denied, your share of the space they attack.

        The model has no offside, pressing or passing-lane value, so defensive suggestions lean towards dropping deeper. Validated on four matches: higher space denied came before fewer final-third entries.
