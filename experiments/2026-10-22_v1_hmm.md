# v1 — dead ball + sticky possession (HMM)

## Idea
- Dead ball: low team activity (2 s & 5 s), a ball that goes out is treated as dead until the
  restart (ball placed, then kicked), ball missing for a long time while players are slow, start of a set piece
  in Smart Tagging as a weak hint.
- Possession: the team whose players are closer to the ball, weighted by how close.
- Decoder: 3-state HMM H/A/D, Viterbi per half. Parameters from football reasoning
  (average possession ~10 s, stoppage ~15 s), not from tuning on the public GT.

## Results

| | v0 | v1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public spellF1 (noise ±0.36) | 0.3256 | 0.5125 |
| Public macroF1 | 0.6207 | 0.7597 |
| Possessions in one match | 594 | 239 |
| Average possession duration | 7.2 s | 16.3 s |
| D share (scored frames) | 32.3% | 38.8% |

Public GT: 17 possessions, average 14.2 s. As a rough benchmark (common estimate, not yet
checked against a source), a top-league match has about 55 minutes of ball in play out of ~100 minutes, so a D share
of 35-45% is plausible. The main argument for v1: the number and duration of possessions
over a full match are now realistic, not the public score.

## Visible weaknesses
- Stoppages for fouls (ball stays inside the pitch) are still often read as in play.
- Possession changes: public transF1 0.22, timing still ~4 frames early.
- 17 of 63 ball-out episodes hit the 40 s limit (restart not visible; ball missing).

## Decision
Used as the current submission. Next: ball reconstruction (v2) and whistle detection.
