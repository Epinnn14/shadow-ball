See the space, not just the ball.

Shadow Ball reads where every player stands and shows who controls the dangerous space, where each player could have been, and what to change at half-time. Free, in your browser.

Three ways to use it

Tactics board

Pick two LaLiga 2026/27 sides, a formation and the eleven. Choose a moment (build-up, high press, mid-block, final-third attack, a ball lost high, a low block), then drag players and the ball. The model updates on every move.

  • Rest defence: what you would still hold if the ball were lost now

Match replay

Fifteen minutes of a real match (open data from Metrica Sports, players shown by number) as a bench would see it: the live pitch, the Shadow pitch with each player's best position, pressing traps, fatigue, and from the second half a plan built from the first.

Your own match

Film a game from a high, wide angle. The free Python tools turn the video into tracking on your computer (nothing is uploaded), then open the result here.

  1. python run.py video frame match.mp4, then mark four pitch points on the calibration page (the picture stays in your browser)
  2. python run.py video track match.mp4 --calib calib.json and the commands it prints
  3. Open the clip.json it writes

What the numbers mean

For every spot on the pitch the model asks who would get there first, then weights the spot by how often the ball reaches the box from there. Your share of that weighted space is space owned with the ball and space denied without it.

Does it matter? When the defending side denies the most space, attackers reach the final third within 10 seconds far less often:

What it cannot do yet

  • The model knows nothing about offside, pressure on the ball or passing lanes, so defensive advice leans towards dropping deeper.
  • From video, players are found well (86–89% within 2 m in tests) but dead balls are not, so possession from video is still rough.
  • Squads come from club sites and Wikipedia; a few pages had not been updated this season. Every name can be changed on the board.
  • Chemistry between neighbouring players is our own indicator and has not been checked against results.
