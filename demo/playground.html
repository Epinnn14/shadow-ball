    How to read it

    Dangerous space is pitch control (who gets to each spot first) weighted by how often the ball reaches the box from there, learned from three open Metrica games. With the ball: the share you own, and rest defence, the share you would still hold if you lost it now. Without it: space denied, your share of the space they attack.

    The model has no offside, pressing or passing-lane value, so defensive suggestions lean towards dropping deeper. Validated on four matches: higher space denied came before fewer final-third entries.
