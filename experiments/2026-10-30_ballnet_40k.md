# Ballnet retrained for 40,000 steps (2026-10-30)

## Why

In the 20,000-step run (`ballnet_20261024_2123.json`) early stopping on the held-out half picked
step 19,500 (train half 1) and 20,000 (train half 2): the best step sat at the cap, so the model
was still improving when training stopped. This is measured on ball positions only, with no
possession labels, so raising the cap is not tuning on the public window.

## Configuration

`python run.py ball-train --cv --steps 40000 --batch 512` (now the default), RTX laptop, CUDA,
about 1 h for the two cross-validation folds plus the final model.
Report: `ballnet_20261030_1609.json`.

| | 20k steps | 40k steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best step (train H1 / train H2) | 19,500 / 20,000 (cap) | 39,500 / 37,000 |
| Final model steps (mean) | 19,750 | 38,250 |
| Median error, all gaps (mean of folds) | 2.92 m | 2.81 m |
| 2-10 s gaps | 3.98 m | 3.92 m (interpolation 6.85 m) |
| 10-30 s gaps | 5.56 m | 5.37 m (interpolation 16.96 m) |
| Over 30 s | 6.49 m | 6.20 m (interpolation 28.6 m) |
| Correct nearest team, player within 3 m | 76.6% | 78.2% (interpolation 71.7%) |

## Possession (v4.2 pipeline, unchanged otherwise)

Mask test (`ball-train --train-half 2 --steps 40000`, then `scripts/mask_test.py`, same 8 masks:
v1 identical in both runs):

| v4 | 20k | 40k |
| --- | --- | --- |
| spellF1 mean | 0.473 | **0.502** (better 5, equal 2, worse 1) |
| macroF1 mean | 0.829 | **0.838** (better 6, worse 2) |

Same mask-test numbers on the laptop (CUDA) and in the cloud (CPU) from the same half-2 weights
(sha256 d37b8c36…).

Public window: 0.5455 / 0.900, unchanged. Full match: 279 possessions (283 before), mean 13.0 s,
D 43.1%.
